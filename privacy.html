Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Memonic is a memory-palace app for learning facts and words. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. In short: we only use your data to run the app for you. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who is responsible

Memonic is run by Devin Öh, Sweden, who is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: memonic.apps@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our database provider; we can't see them.
Your palaces, rooms and memoriesThe content you create (room names, words, meanings, sound-alikes, scenes) so the app can show it on any device you sign in on.
Photos you addRoom photos (including photo walks) are stored so they appear in your palace. Photos of notes or pages for the Magic Wand are sent to our AI provider to read the text and are not stored by us.
Pictures made by AIStored so they appear in your rooms.
Practice resultsWhat you typed, whether it was right, and timing, to schedule reviews and show how well you remember. We also use these results, without your identity, to check whether the method works.
Usage countsHow many AI features you used per day or month, to apply fair-use limits and keep costs under control. We also record when a help tutorial is opened.
SettingsYour chosen language and similar preferences.
PurchasesIf you buy Pro or a pack: whether you have an active purchase. Payment itself is handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
Feedback emailsIf you email us, we get your email, your message, and the app version and device type the app adds.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), contract) and, for fair-use limits, security and improving the method with anonymised practice data, our legitimate interest in running a reliable, affordable app (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who helps us (processors)

Supabase: account sign-in, database and file storage. Our project is hosted in the EU (Ireland).

OpenAI: when you use AI features, the words, meanings, room names and photos involved are sent to OpenAI to write memories, read notes and draw pictures. Under OpenAI's API terms this data is not used to train their models and is kept for at most 30 days for abuse monitoring.

Google (Gmail): sends our account emails, such as sign-up and password-reset codes.

Apple / Google and RevenueCat: handle in-app purchases and tell us whether you own Pro or a pack.

Some of these companies are based in the USA. Where data leaves the EU/EEA, it's protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign-in session and a few preferences on your device. "Sounds like" uses your phone's built-in voices, which work on the device.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, your palaces, memories, photos, pictures, practice results and account are deleted right away. Copies in our provider's backups disappear within a short period as those backups expire.

Your rights

You can see and edit your content in the app at any time, and delete your account in Settings → Delete account (or without the app: Delete your account). You also have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Sweden: Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY). To use these rights, email memonic.apps@gmail.com.

Children

Memonic is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

AI content

Memories and pictures are made by AI and can contain mistakes. Memonic is a study aid, not a reference: check important facts, especially medical ones, against reliable sources.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
