Delete your account

You can delete your Memonic account and all of its data at any time, with or without the app.

In the app

Open Memonic → Settings → Delete account, and confirm twice. Everything is removed right away.

Without the app

Email memonic.apps@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Memonic account". We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

Your account and sign-in, your palaces, rooms and memories, your room photos and AI pictures, your practice results, usage counts, reports you sent and any record of packs you own. Nothing is kept, except copies in our database provider's backups, which disappear within a short period as those backups expire.

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions first.
