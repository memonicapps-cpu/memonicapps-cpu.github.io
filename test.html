Help test Memonic

Memonic is a memory-palace study app, and it's in its first test on Google Play. You'll get the app free, with all Pro features, while you test. You need an Android phone and must be 13 or older.

Do all three steps on your phone, signed in with the same Google account that's on your phone. Otherwise Google Play says the app can't be found.

  1. Join the test group

    Tap Join group on the page that opens. Other testers can't see you.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Tap Become a tester. If it says the page isn't available, wait a few minutes after step 1 and try again.

    Become a tester

  3. Download Memonic

    Install it from Google Play like any app. It can take a few minutes before it shows up.

    Get it on Google Play

While you test

Stay in the test for 14 days. Google needs at least 12 testers for 14 days in a row before Memonic can be released to everyone, so please don't tap "Leave the program".

Open the app a few times a week. Try a ready-made pack, build a palace of your own with the Magic Wand, and practise.

Tell me what's confusing or broken. In the app: Settings → Send feedback, or email memonic.apps@gmail.com. Screenshots help a lot. You'll also get two short surveys, after one week and after two.

What's collected while you test is described in the privacy policy. No ads, no tracking.
