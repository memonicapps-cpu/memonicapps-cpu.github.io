Memonic

Remember anything, room by room.

Memonic turns the things you need to learn into vivid scenes placed along a route you already know: your home, your commute, your school. It's the memory-palace method used by memory champions, with AI to help you build it and spaced repetition to keep it.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email memonic.apps@gmail.com or use Settings → Send feedback in the app. We usually answer within a few days.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Delete account. Everything is removed right away. Without the app: see Delete your account.

I didn't get the sign-up or reset code.
Wait a minute and check your spam folder, then tap "Send a new code".

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?
Sign in with the same account. Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account and your Memonic account.
